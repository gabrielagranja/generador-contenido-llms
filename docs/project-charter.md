# Project Charter

Authority: canonical product and project contract.
Bootstrap issue: #37.
Status: approved baseline; unresolved items remain pending.

## Vision

Explore and validate a low-cost, human-in-the-loop workflow that helps a content manager turn incomplete business information into accurate, editable social content for a network of small local businesses.

## Primary user

A digital content manager/copywriter coordinating social communication for 20+ small businesses.

## MVP outcome

A reproducible proof of concept that captures a structured brief, surfaces missing information, generates editable channel-adapted content, retrieves approved business context, previews one Instagram image, supports optional voice interaction, and publishes to one authorized Instagram Professional account only after explicit human confirmation.

## Principles

- Human editorial control.
- Accuracy before speed.
- Evidence-led scope.
- Low-cost and extensible choices.
- Privacy-aware evaluation.
- Traceable decisions and tests.
