# MVP Scope

Authority: canonical in-scope/out-of-scope boundary.
Bootstrap issue: #37.
Last reviewed: 2026-10-04.

## In scope

- One representative or explicitly authorized business.
- Structured brief: topic, audience, platform, business context and optional notes.
- Missing or ambiguous information detection before generation.
- Instagram as the priority generation channel.
- Facebook as the second generation/adaptation channel for small-commerce use cases.
- One Instagram feed-image generation and preview flow.
- Small RAG pipeline over approved business context.
- Optional voice interaction for briefing and revisions.
- One authorized Instagram Professional account.
- Explicit human confirmation before every Instagram publication.
- LinkedIn is not part of the first slice unless a validated B2B need reopens it.

## Out of scope or pending

- Facebook automatic publishing.
- LinkedIn generation or publishing in the first slice.
- Unattended or scheduled publishing.
- Multiple businesses and multi-tenant administration.
- Final hosting provider.
- Exact RAG, image and model-comparison depth required by the academic rubric.
- Docker inclusion, pending Issue #34 decision.
- Any external account access not explicitly authorized.

## Change control

Any scope change requires an Issue, an OpenSpec proposal, explicit acceptance criteria, and human approval before implementation.
